**Blend band / edge smoothing (плавное соединение краёв)**

The ring of width B (default 6, max 12) around a force-prepared plot where the surface is interpolated from the plot's level to the natural terrain with slope ≤ 1, so no square platform with vertical walls appears.

**Synonyms:** skirt (only for the *template's* fixed foundation layers — not the same thing), transition band.
