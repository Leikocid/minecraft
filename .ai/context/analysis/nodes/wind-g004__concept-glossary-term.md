**Forced site preparation (принудительная подготовка участка)**

Stage 3 of the spawn search: script terraforming of the best dry site within 500 blocks — clear natural blocks above a target Y, level the 35×35 plot, fill only shallow voids (≤ D), blend the edges into the terrain. Touches whitelisted natural blocks only; aborts before touching a structure, spawner or crafted block.

**Synonyms:** site prep, forced prep, terraforming.
