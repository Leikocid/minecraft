**Pursuit radius / leash** (радиус преследования)

A 20-block **horizontal** (XZ) circle around the **launch point**: the owner's feet at launch, frozen. The volley ends with `out_of_radius` as soon as the target is outside it. Height is ignored so that the Scythe's own launches cannot abort the volley (`L0-scyt-ad05`, `PURSUIT_RADIUS`).
