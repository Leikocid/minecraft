**Candidate chunk / candidate roll**

The shared four-structure discovery unit: each suitable chunk gets one 5% roll per structure type. For Mini Warden City, a successful roll whose site turns out unsuitable is cancelled outright — it is never retried on a neighboring chunk (contrast with the spawn-area Windmill's forced-placement fallback, which Mini Warden City has no equivalent of). Cite: `L0-strf-r002`, `L0-strf-e003`.
