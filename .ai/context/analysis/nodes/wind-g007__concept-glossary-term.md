**Route cells**

The 1-wide, 2-high walkable path from the Windmill's front door through all floors and the continuous staircase to the access cell of each of the 25 chests. Decay (cobwebs, vines) and solid blocks are forbidden on route cells; the template test checks it by BFS.

**Synonyms:** main route, normal route (§4.1 "нормальный маршрут").
