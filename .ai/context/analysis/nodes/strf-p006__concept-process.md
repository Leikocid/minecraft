# Process — the `strf` probe (Stage-1-style spike on BDS 1.26.51.1)

**Links:** `part_of: ["L0-strf"]` · `is_a: ["process"]`

It runs before any body component starts. It uses a throwaway test template generated by `infr`'s NBT writer: a 9×5×7 box holding 2 chests, 1 `mob_spawner` (`minecraft:zombie`), 1 `sculk_shrieker` with `can_summon=true`, a door, and a stair. The results go into the deviation report and the assumptions.

| # | Question | Method | Dependent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does `structureManager.place` keep the chest and spawner block entities, including the spawner `EntityIdentifier`? | Place, then check that `getBlock().typeId` is correct and a zombie spawns within 60 s with a player nearby. | all; `L0-strf-d002` |
| 2 | Is rotation correct for block states (stairs, doors) and positions? Does the origin stay the min corner for 90/180/270? | Place 4 rotations and compare against `rotateLocal` (`L0-strf-r004`). | all; `L0-strf-as06` |
| 3 | Does a shrieker with `can_summon=true` from a template summon a Warden after 4 warnings? | Scripted vibration spam with a player. | `wrdn` |
| 4 | Does `/loot insert <pos> loot "chests/ancient_city"` (and `bastion_treasure`, `bastion_other`) work through `dimension.runCommand`? | Command result plus container contents. | `loot`, `wrdn`, `bast` |
| 5 | Do a script-set `nameTag` and the tag prevent despawn across a 2 km walk-away, unload, and restart? | Spawn 10 zombie villagers, walk away, restart, return, count. | `wind`, `bast` |
| 6 | Does infinite `fire_resistance` (via `runCommand("effect … infinite 0 true")`, since the script API caps the duration) stop sun damage? Is there visible fire? Does the effect persist after restart? Does a cured villager keep name/effect? | Noon test plus a cure with golden apple + weakness. | `wind` |
| 7 | Time per `place` for a 35×35×30 template. Time per 64 `getBlock` calls. | `Date.now()` deltas. | `L0-strf-p005` |
| 8 | Dynamic-property budget: the largest string accepted per key and the total bytes before a warning or error. | Write growing keys. | `L0-strf-as05` |
| 9 | Is a chunk being loaded detectable? Does `getBlock` return `undefined` or throw in unloaded chunks? Is `dimension.isChunkLoaded` present in 2.10.0? | Probe positions at view-distance + 5 chunks. | `L0-strf-as03` |
| 10 | Does `fillBlocks` with air work across chunk borders in one call, and what are its volume limits? | fill 40×40×30 | `L0-strf-p003` |
| 11 | Does `/tickingarea add` through `runCommand` load chunks 500 blocks away for the spawn search? | Add an area, wait for `isChunkLoaded`. | `wind`; `L0-strf-cx02` |

**Exit.** A markdown report committed as `docs/structures/probe-results.md`. Each item is marked PASS, FAIL (with fallback chosen), or N/A. The ADRs flip from `proposed` to `accepted` according to the results.
