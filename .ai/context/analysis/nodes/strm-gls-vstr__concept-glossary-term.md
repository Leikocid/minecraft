**Visual strike (визуальный удар молнии)**

A lightning effect drawn with particles (spark/flash column) and the thunder-impact sound at one point. No entity is spawned, so it causes no damage, fire, knockback or mob conversion (C-30, `L0-adr-sblt`). The active plays 3 strikes and a passive proc plays 1.

It is **not** a `minecraft:lightning_bolt`. Related: the **trace** (линия ветра и электричества), the particle line drawn along the active's ray.
