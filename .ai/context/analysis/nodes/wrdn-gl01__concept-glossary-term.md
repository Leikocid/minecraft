**Mini Warden City**

The add-on's compact, original Overworld structure styled after vanilla's Ancient City — deepslate architecture, Sculk, a central Reinforced Deepslate monument, 2 natural Sculk Shriekers, 10 chests on the real Ancient City loot table, and a ~5×5 surface sculk marker. Not a shrunken copy of the real Ancient City. Spec source: §13 of the Four Structures doc.

**Synonyms:** Warden City (informal); `wrdn` (node slug).
