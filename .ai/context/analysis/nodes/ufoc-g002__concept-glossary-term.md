**Centre (event centre)**

The integer block position under the target's feet at the moment the arrival starts. It is frozen for the whole event. The hover point, the magnet zone, the arrival notice range and the flight path are all measured from it.

**Not** the target's live position.

**Synonyms:** центр события.
