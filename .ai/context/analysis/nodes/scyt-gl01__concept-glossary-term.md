**Scythe of Calamity** (Коса бедствия)

The second legendary weapon, `andrew:scythe_of_calamity`. It shipped in Stage 3 (0.4.0) and was updated in 0.4.2. It is crafted from 2 golden apples, 2 obsidian and 1 diamond hoe, once per world. It has netherite melee damage and infinite durability. Its Use ability fires 3 virtual homing projectiles at the nearest visible **target**: a player first, otherwise a mob. Key prefix `sc`, abilityKey `scythe_of_calamity`, text prefix `andrew.scythe`.

**Synonyms:** Scythe, Коса. As shipped the item carries hoe tags (`L0-scyt-cx03`).
