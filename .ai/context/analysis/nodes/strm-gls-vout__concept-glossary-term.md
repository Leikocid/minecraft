**Vanilla-output recipe**

A pack recipe whose result is an unmodified `minecraft:` item: here `minecraft:elytra` and `minecraft:totem_of_undying`. There is no craft token, gate, dynamic property or lore, and the result is treated as an ordinary item everywhere (C-31, `L0-adr-sbvr`).

It contrasts with a **craft token** (`andrew:*_crafted`), the intermediate result of a legendary recipe that the gate swaps for the real item.
