**Surface Sculk Marker**

The irregular ~5×5 patch of Sculk/Sculk Vein generated on the real surface directly above a Mini Warden City's center. A locator, not infrastructure — it contains no shaft, ladder, or tunnel. Digging straight down from its center is guaranteed (by template co-design) to reach the city interior.
