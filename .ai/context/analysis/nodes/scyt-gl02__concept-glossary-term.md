**Candidate** (кандидат в цели)

An entity that passes `L0-scyt-r001` at the instant of Use: a valid player other than the owner, or a valid entity with a health component (a mob), in the owner's dimension, ≤ 20 blocks away (3D), not hidden (players only) and visible. Candidates split into the **player tier** and the **mob tier** (see Target tier).
