**Natural Sculk Shrieker**

A Sculk Shrieker that behaves exactly like one generated by vanilla worldgen — full participation in the warning/Warden-summon mechanic (`can_summon`), as opposed to a scripted/decorative one. Mini Warden City places exactly 2, and the spec requires both to read as "natural" even though the city itself is script-placed after chunk generation (see `L0-wrdn-as01`).

**Related:** `can_summon` — the vanilla Sculk Shrieker property controlling whether it may attempt to summon a Warden.
