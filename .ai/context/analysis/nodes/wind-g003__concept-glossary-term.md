**Field guard (полевой Zombie Villager)**

One of the exactly 10 vanilla Zombie Villagers spawned once around a Windmill's wheat fields. Tagged `andrew:guard:<instanceId>`, named (no despawn), permanent fire resistance (sun-immune). Never respawned; curing makes an ordinary Villager.

**Synonyms:** field Zombie Villager, initial guards (§6 "initial guards spawned"). **Not:** Zombie Villagers from the floor-1 spawner.
