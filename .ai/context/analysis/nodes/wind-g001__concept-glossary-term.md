**Windmill (Мельница)**

The abandoned stone-and-wood mill structure: a ~15×15×30 building with a fixed 4-blade rotor, 3 floors, 25 chests and 3 spawners, on a fixed ~35×35 plot of wheat fields, water ditches, paths and a broken fence. Structure id `windmill`, template `andrew:windmill`. Always "Windmill" in EN and "Мельница" in RU, in docs, logs and any user string (§8).

**Synonyms:** mill. **Not:** a vanilla village windmill (there is none in Bedrock).
