**Pre-armour damage (урон до учёта брони)**

The raw damage value given to the engine (10 HP active, 6 HP passive), which armour, toughness, Protection and Resistance then reduce like any `entityAttack` (xasm29, C-29). It is the opposite of **true damage**, a direct health write that skips armour. True damage is used by the Sculk Crossbow (C-28) and is forbidden for the Storm Blade.

**Units:** HP; 2 HP = 1 heart.
