**Reinforced Deepslate Monument**

The central hall's decorative landmark, ≈5 blocks wide × 6–7 blocks tall, built from Reinforced Deepslate. Purely cosmetic: no activation, no portal behavior, no teleportation. Not to be confused with the vanilla Warden-spawning mechanic, which it has no functional link to.
